Module: Understanding Financial Statements

Financial statements are formal records that summarize the financial activities and position of a business. These reports help owners, managers, and investors make informed decisions.

The three main financial statements are:

  • Income Statement – shows profit or loss over a period
  • Balance Sheet – shows assets, liabilities, and equity
  • Cash Flow Statement – tracks cash coming in and out

In ABM, understanding these statements is essential for evaluating business performance and financial health.
